The Project

Where Nature Meets Thoughtful Living

Saventure Sakleshpur Farm Resort is envisioned as a destination that brings together natural surroundings, comfortable holiday homes and spaces for leisure, wellness and social gatherings.

From landscaped gardens and horticulture areas to a welcoming clubhouse and peaceful meditation pavilion, the concept emphasizes a relaxed connection with nature.

  • Nature-inspired holiday homes
  • Landscaped gardens and horticulture
  • Clubhouse and dining facilities
  • Meditation and wellness spaces
  • Recreational and gathering areas
